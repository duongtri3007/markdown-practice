# My Favourite Sport
**Football is the world's favorite sport**. Its goal is simple: *score more goals than the opponent by kicking a ball without using the hands and arms*.
**A successful team** relies on *speed, strategy, and unwavering commitment*.

## Key Features of Football
- Played over two 45-minute halves.
- Refereed by a main official and two linesmen.
- Requires maximum physical endurance.
- The drama of the penalty area.

### Steps to starting a match
1. The two teams enter the pitch.
2. The referee flips a coin for the kick-off decision.
3. The match begins with a kick-off at the center spot.
4. The clock starts running.
